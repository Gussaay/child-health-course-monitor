# Global Health Media Project: Level 1 licence for the Sick & Small Newborn online course

**Eligibility (checked on globalhealthmedia.org, 21 Nov 2026):** Sudan is on GHMP's list of high-mortality countries.
Organisations there are eligible for a **Level 1 licence at no charge** when they do not charge for the training
(Ministries of Health, public hospitals, public teaching institutions). The course is free for health workers, so the
National Child Health Program qualifies. Their terms allow an online course to **embed** the videos within the course,
used **whole** (no clipping or changes), and not offered for separate download.

## Step 1: Register (about 5 minutes)

1. Go to **globalhealthmedia.org/registration** ("Sign up to download our videos").
2. Choose the user group: **no charge: organisations using the videos only in lower-income regions, learners not charged**.
3. Organisation: *Federal Ministry of Health, Sudan, National Child Health Program*. Country: *Sudan*.
   Use: *Free online training course for health workers (Sick & Small Newborn), and face-to-face courses*.
4. After registering, on any video page (for example *Keeping The Baby Warm*) choose
   **Mobile, 25–50 MB (Org Level 1) – $0.00** and check out. This downloads the file under the licence.

## Step 3: Start times, automatically

With the licence, download the **25 videos** (Mobile, Org Level 1) and put them in one Google Drive folder (or upload them
to this session). `scripts/ssnb-upload/build/skilltimes.py` then transcribes them on our side (open-source Whisper; the
files are not shared anywhere) and finds where each skill starts. The times go into the lessons as "jump to the skill"
buttons by an in-place update that keeps learner progress. Managers can also set or correct a time in the app: open the
lesson in Manage mode, play the video, and press **Mark the skill start here**.
